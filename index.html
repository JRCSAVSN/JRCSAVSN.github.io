Welcome to my website!

My name's José Reinaldo and I'm a postdoctoral researcher at the Intelligence Sensing Lab, The University of Osaka.

My research focuses on computational imaging, connecting optics and computational algorithms to solve various inverse problems.

Research interests
  • Compressive sensing
  • Lensless imaging
  • Digital holography
  • Video snapshot compressive imaging

01 / WHAT I EXPLORE

Research interests.

Recovering more from less: combining physical models and computational tools to rethink how images are captured and reconstructed.

01

Lensless imaging

Inverse reconstruction without conventional lenses, using optical modeling and data-driven priors to recover visual information.

Inverse problemsComputational optics
02

Compressive video sensing

Recovering dynamic scenes from coded measurements, with an emphasis on efficient, physics-aware reconstruction methods.

Compressive sensingMachine learning
03

Digital holography

Exploring coded multi-wavelength holographic measurements and computational approaches to phase and object-height recovery.

HolographyPhase retrieval/unwrapping
